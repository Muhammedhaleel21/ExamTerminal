=== Data Mining: Review Questions ===

Q1: What is the difference between Data Mining and Data Warehousing?
A1: A Data Warehouse stores consolidated, historical data from multiple sources. Data Mining is the analytical process of extracting patterns and knowledge from that data.

Q2: How does the Apriori principle reduce the search space in association rule mining?
A2: Any subset of a frequent itemset must also be frequent. If an itemset is infrequent, all of its supersets will also be infrequent and can be pruned.

Q3: Differentiate between agglomerative and divisive hierarchical clustering.
A3: Agglomerative is bottom-up (starts with individual points and merges clusters), whereas Divisive is top-down (starts with one large cluster and recursively splits).
