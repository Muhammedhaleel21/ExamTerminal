=== Data Mining: Module 1 ===
Data Preprocessing, Exploration, and Association Rules

1. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:
   - Handling Missing Values: Mean/Median imputation, KNN imputation, drop rows.
   - Outlier Detection: Z-Score, Interquartile Range (IQR), Isolation Forest.
   - Data Transformation: Min-Max Normalization, Z-Score Standardization, One-Hot Encoding.

2. Association Rule Mining:
   - Support, Confidence, and Lift.
   - Apriori Algorithm: Frequency itemset generation and join step.
   - FP-Growth (Frequent Pattern Tree) algorithm for large transaction databases.
