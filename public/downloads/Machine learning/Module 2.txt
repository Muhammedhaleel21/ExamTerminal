=== Machine Learning: Module 2 ===
Neural Networks and Deep Learning Fundamentals

1. Artificial Neural Networks (ANN):
   - Perceptron: Inputs, weights, bias, activation function.
   - Activation Functions: Sigmoid, ReLU (Rectified Linear Unit), Leaky ReLU, Tanh, Softmax.
   - Forward Propagation: Matrix multiplication of inputs and layer weights.

2. Backpropagation and Optimization:
   - Loss Functions: Mean Squared Error (MSE), Cross-Entropy Loss.
   - Gradient Descent: Batch GD, Stochastic GD (SGD), Adam Optimizer.
   - Learning Rate Scheduling and Momentum.

3. Deep Learning Architectures:
   - Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) for computer vision.
   - Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN) and LSTMs for sequential data.
   - Transformer architectures for Natural Language Processing.
